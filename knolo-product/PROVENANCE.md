# Knolo product runtime provenance

This directory was imported into the Knolo workspace from the source revision
recorded in [`SOURCE_REV`](SOURCE_REV). It is being adapted into the Knolo
product under the terms that apply to each source component.

- First-party source remains covered by the applicable Apache-2.0 license and
  copyright notices.
- Vendored dependencies retain their original licenses and notices.
- Adapted source must retain required attribution and identify substantive
  changes in release documentation.
- Provider credentials, upstream accounts, upstream telemetry defaults, and
  upstream installation instructions are not part of Knolo’s product contract.

Before a component is published, record its disposition in the migration plan’s
provenance register and include the relevant notice in the release bundle.
